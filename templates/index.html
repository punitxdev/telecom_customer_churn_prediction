About This Project

Customer Behaviour Analyzer is an advanced machine learning application designed to help telecommunications companies proactively identify customers at risk of leaving.

Leveraging a robust XGBoost classifier, this tool analyzes 31 distinct customer features—spanning demographic data, account information, and subscribed services—to compute a precise churn probability score. Based on this score, the system categorizes customers into three risk tiers to help prioritize retention efforts:

  • Low Risk (< 30%): Stable account, no immediate action required.
  • Moderate Risk (30% - 69%): Warning stage. Customer behavior indicates they may leave. Consider offering tailored incentives.
  • High Risk (≥ 70%): Critical risk. High likelihood of churning. Requires immediate contact and targeted retention strategies.
